Shurui Li 李书睿

Ph.D. student in Computer Science at ShanghaiTech University, studying multimodal AI and the human brain.

I am advised by Yuanning Li. My research explores how information is represented across vision, language, and the human brain, and how representations in artificial systems relate to neural activity. I develop datasets and computational models to study these connections, including work with image–text pairs, fMRI recordings, and auditory-induced visual imagery. I am especially interested in brain decoding and in building benchmarks that help compare what multimodal models learn with what the brain encodes.

Research

I am interested in how representations connect across modalities and between artificial and biological systems. My current research centers on:

  • Multimodal representation learning across vision, language, and neural signals
  • Brain decoding and brain–computer interfaces
  • Datasets and benchmarks for neural and multimodal AI
